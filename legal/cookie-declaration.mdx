## What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and for marketing purposes. Below you will find detailed information about the specific cookies we use.

## Types of Cookies We Use

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website.

## Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control and manage cookies in various ways:

### Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner where you can: - **Accept All**: Consent to all cookie categories - **Reject Non-Essential**: Only allow technically necessary cookies - **Customize**: Choose specific cookie categories

### Cookie Settings

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by: 1. Clicking the cookie settings button (gear icon) at the bottom right of any page 2. Adjusting your preferences in the cookie customization modal 3. Saving your new preferences

### Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can set your browser to: - Accept all cookies - Reject all cookies - Notify you when a cookie is set - Delete cookies when you close your browser

**Browser-specific instructions:** - **Firefox**: <a href="https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Enhanced Tracking Protection</a> - **Chrome**: <a href="https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=en&amp;co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Manage Cookies</a> - **Safari**: Third-party cookies are blocked by default - **Edge**: <a href="https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Delete Cookies</a> - **Opera**: <a href="https://help.opera.com/en/latest/web-preferences/#cookies" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Cookie Preferences</a> - **Brave**: <a href="https://support.brave.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022806212-How-do-I-manage-cookies-" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Manage Cookies</a>

## Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly:

**Essential Cookies**: Disabling these will significantly impact website functionality: - You may need to re-enter information more frequently - Form submissions may not work properly - Security features may be compromised

**Analytics Cookies**: Disabling these will not affect website functionality but: - We cannot measure website performance and user experience - We cannot identify areas for improvement

**Marketing Cookies**: Disabling these will not affect website functionality but: - You may see less relevant advertisements - We cannot measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns
