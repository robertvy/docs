Use this guide if you already own numbers in Sipgate and want them to ring through itellicoAI.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Sipgate SIP credentials">
    1. Log in to [Sipgate](https://app.sipgate.com)
    2. Go to your phone number settings
    3. Enable SIP trunk access for your number
    4. Note the SIP registrar address and credentials
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create SIP Trunk in itellicoAI">
    Go to **Telephony → SIP Trunks → Create SIP Trunk**:
    - **Name:** Sipgate
    - **Termination URI:** Sipgate's SIP registrar address
    - **SIP Auth Username/Password:** Your Sipgate SIP credentials
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure call forwarding in Sipgate">
    Set up call forwarding on your Sipgate number to route inbound calls to the itellicoAI SIP endpoint.
  </Step>
</Steps>
