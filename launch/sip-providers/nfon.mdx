Use this guide if your business numbers already live in NFON.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get NFON SIP credentials">
    1. Log in to the [NFON admin portal](https://portal.nfon.com)
    2. Navigate to your SIP trunk settings
    3. Note the SIP registrar, username, and password
    4. Configure an outbound route for the numbers you want to forward
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create SIP Trunk in itellicoAI">
    Go to **Telephony → SIP Trunks → Create SIP Trunk**:
    - **Name:** NFON
    - **Termination URI:** NFON's SIP registrar address
    - **SIP Auth Username/Password:** Your NFON SIP credentials
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure forwarding in NFON">
    Set up call forwarding on your NFON numbers to route calls to the itellicoAI SIP endpoint (`sip:50ebbp4vhpr.eu.sip.livekit.cloud`).
  </Step>
</Steps>
