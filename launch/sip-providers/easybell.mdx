Use this guide when your existing DACH phone numbers already run through Easybell.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Easybell SIP credentials">
    1. Log in to your [Easybell](https://www.easybell.de) customer portal
    2. Navigate to your SIP trunk settings
    3. Note the SIP registrar, username, and password
    4. Ensure the trunk supports external routing
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create SIP Trunk in itellicoAI">
    Go to **Telephony → SIP Trunks → Create SIP Trunk**:
    - **Name:** Easybell
    - **Termination URI:** Easybell's SIP registrar address
    - **SIP Auth Username/Password:** Your Easybell SIP credentials
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure routing">
    In Easybell, configure call forwarding to route inbound calls to the itellicoAI SIP endpoint.
  </Step>
</Steps>
